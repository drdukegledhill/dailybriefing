# About the reader

This text goes to the AI every morning so it knows what matters to you.
Edit it freely: the more specific you are, the better the briefing.

- I work in UK higher education with a focus on **engineering, computer science and games** provision.
- I care about: student recruitment and admissions trends, OfS regulation and quality, funding and finances
  (including international students and fees), curriculum and accreditation (Engineering Council, BCS,
  Ukie/TIGA links for games), graduate employability and industry partnerships, research funding
  (UKRI, EPSRC, Innovate UK), and the impact of AI on teaching, learning and assessment.
- Games: I'm interested in the UK games industry mainly as it affects education — jobs, skills
  shortages, studio openings and closures, tax relief and funding, and esports in education.
  General games reviews and consumer news are not useful to me.
- Prefer UK news. Include international stories only when they are likely to affect UK universities.
- Tone: concise, plain English, no hype. Tell me why something matters, not just what happened.
